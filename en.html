Study at Ural Federal University

Ekaterinburg, Russia. Bachelor’s, Specialist’s and Master’s programs, a preparatory department for the Russian language, and support from the day you arrive.

Applications for Bachelor’s and Master’s programs: June 20 to August 20.

Write to admission@urfu.ru Study in Russia portal Starting September 1, an additional admission round for tuition-based Bachelor’s and Master’s programs is open.

How to apply

Five steps from choosing a program to your first day in Ekaterinburg.

  1. Choose a program

    Bachelor’s programs are taught in Russian, 15 Master’s programs are taught in English.

    Programs and fees

  2. Send documents

    June 20 to August 20. Passport, previous education documents and transcript, with notarized Russian translations.

    admission@urfu.ru

  3. Entrance tests

    Bachelor’s: two tests in major subjects and a Russian language test, level B1–B2. Master’s: an online interview.

    Requirements

  4. Contract and visa

    Sign the contract and pay tuition by August 21. With the invitation letter, apply for a visa at the Russian embassy.

    Visa and enrolment

  5. Arrival

    Tell the university about your arrival at least 10 days before the date of entry using the arrival form.

    Arrival form

Documents issued abroad need legalization or an apostille, otherwise the Admissions Office cannot consider them. Notarized translations into Russian are required whatever the language of the program.

Programs and fees

Bachelor’s and Specialist’s

144 programs taught in Russian. Bachelor’s takes 4 years, Specialist’s 5–6 years. Tuition for non-CIS citizens: 320 000 to 507 000 RUB per year.

Full list with fees

Preparatory department

Russian as a foreign language, and a 1.5-year foundation year: Russian plus specialty subjects. Open to everyone over 17.

Scholarships

The Russian Government Scholarship covers tuition for a number of international students each year. The Open Doors olympiad gives admission to Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD programs.

First steps in Ekaterinburg

The main campus is at 19 Mira St. From the railway station it is a taxi ride of about 250 rubles, or trams 8, 22, 23, 15 to the “Ural Federal University” stop.

  1. Submit your documents

    Office I-317 in the main building, 19 Mira St.

  2. Medical examination and fingerprinting

    Required by law for all foreign citizens within 90 days of arrival.

  3. Health insurance

    A certificate valid in Russia for the whole academic year, with a Russian translation if it is in English.

  4. Medical check for the dormitory

    UrFU hospital, 59b Komsomolskaya St. The examination is paid.

  5. Check in to the dormitory

    Bring your passport, confirmation of studies, the medical certificate and the insurance to office I-110.

  6. Pay for tuition and housing

    There is a Sberbank office in the main building, 19 Mira St.

Dormitory No. 5 at 144 Malysheva St., a 15-minute walk from the main building, is for international students: 1 000 places, residents from more than 60 countries. All buildings and dormitories on the campus page (in Russian).
